How MUNKA Works

A simple approach to organised piggery management.

1Set Up Your Farm

The farm owner or administrator arranges the farm account and creates or assigns authorised users according to their responsibilities.

2Register Your Pigs

Record pig identification, breed, sex, source and other required details in the management system.

3Record Farm Activities

Authorised users enter relevant information about gestation, farrowing, weaning, feeding, treatments, sales and expenses.

4Review Farm Information

Use the available dashboard and reports to review recorded activities and financial information. The quality of the results depends on accurate, complete records.

5Manage Your Farm

Use the available records to help plan farm activities, monitor performance and make better-informed decisions.
