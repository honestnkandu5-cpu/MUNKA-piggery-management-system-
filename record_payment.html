Record Subscription Payment

Use this page to record payments received manually from farm customers through MTN, Airtel or other approved payment methods.

Payment Details

How Manual Payment Recording Works

  1. Customer pays the subscription manually.
  2. Confirm the payment and transaction reference.
  3. Select the customer's farm.
  4. Select the subscription plan.
  5. Select MTN, Airtel or the appropriate payment method.
  6. Enter the transaction reference.
  7. Click Save Payment.
  8. The system records the payment and updates the subscription.
