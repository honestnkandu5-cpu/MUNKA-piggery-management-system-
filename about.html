About MUNKA PIGGERY

Bringing smarter record management to pig farming.

Who We Are

MUNKA PIGGERY TECHNOLOGY provides a digital piggery management system designed to help farmers organise farm records and monitor important farm activities.

The system brings relevant farm information together so authorised users can record activities and review the information available to them.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to make piggery record management more organised and accessible. We aim to help farm owners understand what is happening on their farms, including when they are away from the farm.

Our Vision

To support modern pig farming through organised digital records, improved monitoring and informed farm management decisions.
