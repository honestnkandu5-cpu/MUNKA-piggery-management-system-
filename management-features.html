Powerful Piggery Management Features

MUNKA helps pig farmers organise farm records, monitor pig production, manage daily activities and understand their farm's financial performance, even when they are away from the farm.

🐖

Pig Registration

Register pigs and keep important information such as pig identification, breed, sex, source, farrowing date, weight and health status.

🤰

Gestation Management

Record service dates and monitor important pregnancy milestones, expected farrowing dates and scheduled management activities.

🐷

Farrowing Records

Record farrowing events and track litter information to help monitor sow performance and piglet production.

🍼

Weaning Management

Keep records of weaning activities and piglet numbers to support better monitoring of production.

💊

Health and Treatment

Record vaccinations, treatments, veterinary interventions and other health activities to help maintain organised animal health records.

🌽

Feeding Management

Record feeding activities and monitor feed use to help farmers understand feeding practices and manage resources.

💰

Sales Management

Record pig and other farm sales, helping you keep track of sales transactions and income.

🧾

Expense Tracking

Record farm expenses so you can review where money is being spent and make more informed financial decisions.

📊

Reports and Dashboard

Review available farm records, production information and financial summaries through the system's reporting dashboard.

🔐

User Roles and Permissions

Assign staff roles and control access according to configured permissions, helping organise responsibilities within the farm.

🏡

Farm Management

Organise records for your farm and support more structured management of daily farm operations.

📝

Activity Records

Keep track of recorded activities to support accountability and make it easier to review farm operations.
