Welcome to MUNKA PIGGERY TECHNOLOGY

MUNKA PIGGERY TECHNOLOGY is designed to help pig farmers manage their farms in a more organised, informed and efficient way.

Whether you manage a small piggery or a growing commercial farm, keeping accurate records is important for understanding production, controlling expenses and making better farming decisions.

MUNKA brings important farm information together in one management system, helping authorised farm users record activities and allowing farm owners to monitor operations more conveniently.

Why Choose MUNKA?

Make Your Piggery Easier to Manage

Organise everyday farming activities and use farm records to support better decisions.

🐖

Know Your Pigs

📅

Monitor Production

Record breeding and gestation activities and follow important dates throughout the production process.

Track Pig Health

Keep records of feeding, vaccination, treatment and other important animal health activities.
