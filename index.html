Welcome to MUNKA PIGGERY TECHNOLOGY

Managing a pig farm involves more than feeding pigs. Farmers need to monitor breeding, pregnancy, farrowing, piglet growth, animal health, sales and expenses.

MUNKA PIGGERY TECHNOLOGY is designed to help bring these important records together in one organised management system.

Whether you manage your farm personally or supervise operations while away, organised records can help you understand what is happening on your farm.

Better records. Better monitoring. More informed farming decisions.
